Certificate designs

Ten takes on the Mindlytics Academy certificate. Each one is a standalone HTML file at A4 landscape — open it, click any text to edit the name, course, date or signatures, then print to PDF.

01 · Emerald Classic

The original: deep emerald ground, folded gold ribbon corners, navy Playfair.

02 · Royal Navy Deco

Art-deco stepped corner fans and a double frame on midnight navy.

03 · Burgundy Academy

Traditional academic look: triple rule frame, jewel corners, rose-gold accents.

04 · Ivory Minimal

No ornament but one hairline frame; all the weight is in the type.

05 · Midnight Champagne

Dark full-bleed card, champagne brackets, light type — looks premium on screen.

06 · Tech Gradient

Sans display, geometric planes, cyan-violet gradient — fits AI and data courses.

07 · Platinum Noir

Black and silver, wide-tracked Cinzel caps, four-corner brackets.

08 · Imperial Purple

Baroque scroll corners and a plum ground — the most decorative of the set.

09 · Cairo Gold — Arabic

Right-to-left Arabic layout with eight-point star lattice corners.

10 · Mindlytics Modern

White page, brand-green side band, sans caps — the most corporate of the set.
